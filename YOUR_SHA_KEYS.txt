================================================================================
HOW TO ADD THESE TO FIREBASE CONSOLE:
================================================================================

1. Go to: https://console.firebase.google.com/
2. Select your project: "kisanone-official"
3. Click ⚙️ Settings icon → Project settings
4. Scroll down to "Your apps" section
5. Find your Android app (package: com.kisanone.KisanOne)
6. Click "Add fingerprint" button
7. Add BOTH SHA-1 and SHA-256 keys (paste them one at a time)
8. Click "Save"

================================================================================
IMPORTANT: After adding SHA keys
================================================================================

1. Download the updated google-services.json from Firebase Console
2. Replace both files:
   - C:\kisanone-frontend-main\google-services.json
   - C:\kisanone-frontend-main\android\app\google-services.json

3. Rebuild your app:
   npx expo run:android
